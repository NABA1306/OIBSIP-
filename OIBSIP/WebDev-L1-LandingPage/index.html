Three farms, three cups

Each origin is roasted on its own schedule, by hand, to the curve that suits it — not a house default applied to everything.

Finca Soledad

Huila, Colombia

Washed caturra, grown at 1,850m. Bright and clean, with a stone-fruit finish that holds up in milk.

Gedeb Highlands

Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia

Natural-processed heirloom. Floral up front, jammy berry through the middle, long and sweet.

Mount Kenya Co-op

Nyeri, Kenya

SL28 and SL34, washed. Blackcurrant acidity and a heavy, syrupy body — a roast for black coffee drinkers.

From cherry to cup, on paper

We log every lot — moisture reading, roast curve, cupping score — and print it on the bag. No mystery blends.

Cherry

Green

Roast
